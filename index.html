Honey Barrel Hunter

Here, whiskey and analytics collide.
Honey Barrel Hunter is an independent whiskey evaluation project focused on blind tasting and disciplined scoring of single-barrel and store picks.

  • Blind flights reduce label and price bias.
  • Repeat tastings prevent one-pour distortion.
  • Transparent scoring across nose, palate, and finish.
  • Independent methodology — no paid placements.
